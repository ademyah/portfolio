Profile

I am an embedded systems and IoT engineer based in Tunis, with an engineering degree from ISI Manar (2026) and a bachelor's degree from ISSAT Gafsa (2023). My work covers the full chain of an embedded product: hardware design, firmware, embedded Linux, communication and applications on top.

My recent work focuses on embedded vision and edge AI. For my final year project at Capgemini Engineering, I took an ADAS object detection pipeline from model training (YOLOv8 with knowledge distillation) to deployment on FPGA and Jetson Nano, including precision reduction and quantization analysis.

I am rigorous, autonomous and organized, and I work well in a team to reach shared goals. I am open to embedded, IoT and edge AI opportunities.
